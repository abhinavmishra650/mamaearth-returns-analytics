Situation

Mamaearth’s regional performance requires an accurate view of operational
demand. Our raw (pre-cleaning) total revenue stood at Rs.99,860.20, but
accounting for transactional anomalies establishes our cleaned total revenue at
Rs.97,358.30. Core sales trends were previously obscured by anomalous wholesale
activity: January 2026 looked like Rs.29,582.10 but is actually Rs.11,637.10
once the two bulk-order outliers are excluded. With these adjustments, the true
peak month once bulk-order outliers are excluded was March 2026 at Rs.20,318.90.

Complication

Operational efficiency and margins are severely undermined by order returns and
data-capture issues. Looking at customer behavior, the return rate by payment
method demonstrates significant variance: COD 44.4%, UPI 18.9%, and CARD 14.7%.
This fulfillment risk is heavily localized, with our highest-risk segment being
COD orders in city_tier 2 cities, where 54.5% returned. Compounding these
fulfillment losses, data-pipeline errors generated a duplicate-order
reconciliation delta of Rs.2,501.90, artificially inflating our uncleaned
revenue figures and complicating financial close.

Resolution

Regional operations and finance leaders should take immediate joint action this
week:

  - Implement mandatory customer pre-dispatch confirmation for COD orders in
    city_tier 2 cities to mitigate reverse-logistics costs, while driving
    customer adoption toward prepaid payment methods.
  - Audit ingestion pipelines between our checkout portal and core ledger to
    prevent order duplication and book the duplicate-order reconciliation delta.
  - Align regional inventory planning and dispatch staffing to baseline demand
    patterns established in March 2026, avoiding resource misallocation caused
    by outlier-inflated sales figures from January 2026.
