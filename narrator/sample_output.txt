Situation
Mamaearth's Growth Analytics team closed the books on this order set at Rs.97,358.30 in verified, cleaned revenue, after removing data-entry noise from the raw Rs.99,860.20 extract.

Complication
Two issues are eating into that number. First, a batch of duplicate order submissions inflated the raw revenue figure by Rs.2,501.90 -- a data-quality gap, not real demand -- which is why the cleaned total sits below the raw one. Second, and commercially more urgent: returns are concentrated, not evenly spread. Cash-on-Delivery (COD) orders return at 44.4%, versus 14.7% for Card and 18.9% for UPI -- and within COD the risk is not uniform either: COD orders in city_tier 2 cities return at 54.5%, the single highest-risk segment in the business. Separately, one month that looked like the revenue peak was an artifact: January 2026 appeared to lead at Rs.29,582.10, but once two outsized bulk orders are excluded it falls to Rs.11,637.10, revealing March 2026 as the true peak month at Rs.20,318.90.

Resolution
Regional ops should prioritize tightening COD acceptance in city_tier 2 markets first -- that segment alone returns at 54.5%, well above the 44.4% blended COD rate, so a targeted fix (partial prepayment, delivery-time verification, or COD caps on high-return SKUs) will move the number further than a blanket COD policy change. Finance should treat the Rs.2,501.90 duplicate-order gap as a checkout/submission bug to fix at the source, not a revenue loss to absorb, and should re-baseline monthly targets on March 2026's Rs.20,318.90 rather than the outlier-inflated January 2026 figure, so future forecasts are not anchored to a one-off spike.
